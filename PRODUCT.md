# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, static export friendly) + TypeScript, chosen by the user.
Remotion (`@remotion/player`, `@remotion/three`) runs the logo intro and the 3D
resin object live in the browser; the same compositions can be rendered to MP4
for Instagram.

## Users

- People in Azerbaijan furnishing or gifting for a home: they found Daisy & Leo on
  Instagram and want to see the full range, sizes and prices, then message to order.
- Gift buyers (weddings, Ramadan/Novruz, housewarming) choosing a boxed piece.
- Companies ordering commissioned pieces with their own motif (the wind-turbine
  plaque is a corporate commission).

## Product Purpose

Showcase and catalogue for **Daisy & Leo Design**, a studio hand-making luxury home
decor in epoxy resin: wall and desk clocks, mirrors, photo frames, Islamic
calligraphy panels and commissioned plaques. Success = a visitor finds a piece,
sees its price, and opens a WhatsApp/Instagram conversation about it.

## Positioning

Every piece is poured by hand, so no two are identical: real stone (green
aventurine, clear quartz, obsidian), gold leaf veining, mirror-gold acrylic
numerals and calligraphy, sculpted flowers, presented in an engraved wooden box
with the branded satin ribbon. Made to order in custom colours and sizes.

## Operating Context

Orders happen in conversation (WhatsApp / Instagram DM), not a checkout. Prices are
shown; the inquiry message is pre-filled with the piece name.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: **Azerbaijani first**, English toggle. Fonts must cover ə ı ş ğ ç ö ü İ.
- Prices: shown per piece (₼). **Current prices in `src/data/pieces.ts` are
  placeholders** until the studio supplies real ones.
- Contacts: **placeholders** (WhatsApp number, city) until supplied. Instagram
  handle assumed `@daisyleodesign` from the stand plaque in photo 04.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Daisy & Leo Design (short mark "D&L"). Packaging line: "The Luxury";
  frame line: "Luxury Home Decor Collection".
- Logo: lion head in profile formed from flame-like mane strokes, a five-petal
  flower (daisy) at its centre; gold-to-burgundy fill on black.
- User-pinned palette: dark/obsidian, ivory with a creamy warmth, emerald green,
  plus the mirror gold the pieces are made with.
- User-pinned bans: no AI slop, no typical fonts, no decorative gradients.

## Evidence on Hand

`references/images/` — 9 Instagram photos: agate wall clock (+detail), camellia
mirror (x2), graphite calligraphy panel, emerald calligraphy gift set, wind-turbine
plaque, desk clock + photo frame, logo. No testimonials, press, or client list —
do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads; the interface recedes.
2. Material truth: show stone, gold leaf and resin depth up close.
3. One action per piece: ask about it.
4. Azerbaijani reads as the original, not a translation.
