## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the Daisy & Leo gift box itself — engraved walnut lid, black satin ribbon with gold type, pieces lifted out onto ivory tissue. Refuses the black-and-gold luxury template (dark hero + gold gradient + equal card grid).

OWN-WORLD: Ivory tissue ground (#f0eadb), walnut-stained wood with engraved (burnt, debossed) type as structural surfaces, black satin ribbon (#171112) carrying flat gold type, emerald lining (#173830) as one committed chapter, mirror gold only as flat cut metal (#c9a961), mocha/sand for secondary ink. Display: Gloock; text/UI: Hanken Grotesk. Fitted-insert compartments (thin walnut rules) structure the catalogue.

STORY: Visitor sees the box open and a real piece rise out of it, understands every piece is hand-poured with real stone and gold leaf, browses the fitted catalogue with prices, and taps "Soruş" to ask on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Remotion intro (lion mark draws in gold, flower blooms, ribbon crosses, lid lifts) hands off to the hero: a 3D engraved walnut box (three.js via @remotion/three) centred-right on ivory tissue, lid hinging open with scroll and the Sahil clock rising out; headline left at display scale; primary action "Kolleksiyaya bax" + WhatsApp beside it; satin ribbon crosses the bottom edge into the next section.
